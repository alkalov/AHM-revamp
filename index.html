How to Use the Anti-Hope Machine

Step 1 — Your Real Numbers

Fill in Overall Trading Stats from your broker. Powers your session grades. Leave blank to just explore.

Step 2 — Set Your Strategy

Two groups per tab:

  • Core Settings — profit target, stop loss, win rate, trades/day, share goal.
  • Realistic Conditions — spread, slippage, commission, pullback, entry offset, discipline, bad/good days. These expose the gap between paper and live performance.

Each card has a toggle. OFF = that variable has zero effect on the sim.

Step 3 — Daily % Override

In Realistic Conditions, enable Daily % Override to bypass all tick math. Set min/max daily return %. Each sim day draws randomly from that range. Day% column shows the result.

Step 4 — Simulate

Hit ▶ SIMULATE. Get an equity curve, day-by-day table, and a plain-English verdict: Viable, Marginal, or Failing.

Step 5 — Compare Strategies

Strategy B and Strategy C tabs run independently. Edge diagnostics always reflect the active tab.

Step 6 — Log Sessions

Enter today's results in Today's Session. After a few days you'll get a grade (S–F) vs your own average.

Step 7 — Export

⬇ XLSX exports all settings, edge diagnostics, and the full table as CSV (opens in Excel).

Key Terms

  • Tick — smallest price move. US stocks: $0.01/share.
  • R:R — Reward:Risk ratio. 1.67:1 = win 1.67 ticks per tick risked.
  • Edge — expected profit per trade after all costs. Positive = works.
  • Friction — commission + slippage + spread per trade.
  • Pullback — price retraces after entry. If it exceeds your target, the win becomes a stop-out.
  • Entry Offset — ticks lost by entering late. Shrinks your effective target immediately.
